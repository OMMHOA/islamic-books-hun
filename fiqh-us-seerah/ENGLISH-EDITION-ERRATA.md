# English-edition errata

Errors in the **IIFSO English translation** of *Fiqh-us-Seerah* (Revised 2nd Edition,
1420/1999, distributed by IIPH), verified against the **Arabic original** (Maṭābiʿ al-Shurūq
ed., 368 pp; printed page = PDF page + 1). The Arabic is the sole source of truth (fidelity
policy, 2026-07-16): every item below has been **corrected in both `-ENG-full.md` and the
Hungarian translation**, except §9, where the Arabic itself errs.

Found in four passes: the chunk-aligned HUN↔Arabic verification (2026-07-17), the name and
ḥadīth-grade sweep (2026-07-18), the user's read-through (2026-07-20 →) and a complete
HUN↔Arabic re-read (2026-09-24/25). Locations: "ENG ~NNNN" = line in
`FiqhusSeerah-Muhammad-al-Ghazali-ENG-full.md` when the item was found (lines drift — search
for the quoted text); "p.N" / "AR p.N" = printed page of the Arabic edition. Names are
given in the English edition's transliteration.

## 4. Additions and softenings — removed

The Arabic has no such wording:

- "Saudi Arabian countryside" → **"our countryside"** (ريفنا, ch5 p.136); "Saudi children" →
  **"our children"** (أطفالنا, ch2 p.50); "Saudi villages" → **"our villages"** (قرانا, ch7
  p.270).
- "(and what a life!)" interpolation (ch9 p.362).
- Ibn Qamī'ah's line in Abū Sufyān's Uḥud exchange; Q1:5 (the Arabic quotes only 1:6–7);
  "likes and dislikes" (Preface); "cunning" before "Jews" (ch7). (Scan-verified where the
  Arabic transcription was unclear.)

Softenings (user decision 2026-09-25: the author is rendered as bluntly as he wrote):

- ch6 «هذه خلال اليهود» — "the character traits of **some of** the Jews" → "of the Jews".
- ch7 «فهم إلى اليوم دهاقين الربا فى العالم وهم قادة التبرج والعهر» — "are **among** the masters
  of usury… and **some** are the leaders…" → unqualified, as in the Arabic (the author's own
  qualifier in the next sentence is kept).
- ch6 «يابن مقطعة البظور» — "son of the woman who circumcises girls" → "son of the woman who
  cuts clitorises".
- Epilogue «الصليبية» ×2 — "Christianity" → "the Crusading power".

## 9. Errors in the *Arabic original* — kept, with a translator's note

Both files stay faithful to the Arabic and carry an inline note — ENG `[… — translator's
note]`, HUN `[… – a ford.]`. Where the English already printed the correct form, the correct
form is kept, with the note.

- **Sa'd ibn 'Ubādah, "chief of the Aws"** (AR p.292, «زعيم الأوس») — historically chief of the
  **Khazraj**.
- **Thābit ibn Aqrad / Arqam** — the Arabic prints both («أرقم» p.281, «أقرد» p.282). ENG
  mirrors both with a note at "Aqrad"; HUN uses the historically correct **Arqam**, with a note.
- **Banū 'Abd al-Muṭṭalib** (Ḥilf al-Fuḍūl clan list, AR p.58) — genealogically incoherent;
  the correct **Banū al-Muṭṭalib** is kept.
- **Ibrāhīm's mother "Maryam"** (AR p.347) — misprint of «مريم» for the Coptic **Māriyah**
  («مارية»).
- **Al-Albānī's signature date "1975 هـ"** (AR p.360 fn) — for **1375 AH**.
- **"Abū Ḥibbān"** (ch1, AR p.23) — the Arabic's kunyah for 'Abdullāh ibn Ubayy; the known
  form (Bukhārī) is **Abū Ḥubāb** «أبو حباب».
- **Abān ibn "Sa'd" ibn al-'Āṣ** (ch7, AR p.253) — his father is **Sa'īd** (أبان بن سعيد بن العاص).
- Zayd ibn Ḥārithah printed as «زيد بن ثابت» (ch3, AR p.75); «عمر بن عنبسة» for 'Amr ibn
  'Abasah; «سعيد بن العاص» for Khālid ibn Sa'īd ibn al-'Āṣ; «صعصعة بن مالك» for Mālik ibn
  Ṣa'ṣa'ah; «سعد بن عبادة» at 'Aqabah (Ibn Isḥāq: al-'Abbās ibn 'Ubādah ibn Naḍlah); «دار عمر
  بن ربيعة» (Ibn Isḥāq: the house of the Banū Jaḥsh); the Prophet's parents "who died in
  Medina" (Āminah died at al-Abwā'); «أبو عبادة» for Abū 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrāḥ (AR p.201,
  scan-verified); «عبدالله بن أوفى» for Ibn Abī Awfā; Abū Mūsā «ابن عمه» (the sources: his
  nephew); the Farewell Sermon's «دم ربيعة بن الحارث» (the ḥadīth: the blood of Rabī'ah's son —
  Rabī'ah outlived the Prophet).
